Round or elongated? Measure your toilet in one minute

By Zach Wennstedt · Updated November 10, 2026 · Prices checked November 10, 2026

Measure from the center of the two seat bolts to the front edge of the bowl. About 16 to 17 inches is round. About 18 to 19 inches is elongated. Enter your number below and we'll tell you which seats fit.

Fit checker

How to measure, step by step

  1. Lift the seat and lid.
  2. Find the two bolts that hold the seat on, at the back of the bowl.
  3. Put the end of a tape measure at the midpoint between the bolts.
  4. Measure straight forward to the outside front edge of the bowl rim.
  5. While you're there, measure the distance between the two bolts. Most toilets are about 5.5 inches apart.

Those ranges match what major retailers publish (for example Lowe's guide to measuring a toilet seat). If you land in between, say 17.5 inches, send us a photo with the tape in it and we'll check before you order.

Which shape is better?

RoundElongated
Front-to-back spaceAbout 2 inches shorterNeeds more room
Comfort for adultsFine for short visitsMost adults find it more comfortable
Best forSmall powder rooms, tight doorsMain bathrooms, accessibility, bidet seats

Elongated is generally recommended for accessibility because it gives more seating surface. In a tight room, check the clearance in front of the bowl too: you need room to stand, turn and, if a walker is used, to bring it close.

Don't forget the rough-in

If you're replacing the whole toilet, also measure the rough-in: the distance from the finished wall behind the toilet to the center of the floor bolts. Most homes are 12 inches; some older homes are 10 or 14. A toilet built for the wrong rough-in either won't fit or leaves a gap behind the tank.

Questions people ask

How do I know if my toilet is round or elongated?

Measure from the center between the seat bolts to the front edge of the bowl. Roughly 16 to 17 inches is round, roughly 18 to 19 inches is elongated.

Will an elongated seat fit a round toilet?

No. It will overhang the front of the bowl and won't sit or seal properly. A few oversized heavy-duty seats are designed to cover both shapes, which the product page will say.

Is elongated or round better for seniors?

Elongated, in most cases. The longer bowl gives more seating surface and is easier to use. Choose round only when the room is too tight for the extra length.

What is a toilet rough-in?

The distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain (the floor bolts). Most U.S. homes use 12 inches. It matters when you replace the whole toilet, not when you replace only the seat.
