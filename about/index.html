About Buy Smart Toilet

Buy Smart Toilet is a small independent store run by Zach Wennstedt in Denver, Colorado. We sell smart toilets, bidet seats and accessible toilets, and we write plain guides on choosing them.

Why this store exists

Most toilet and bidet pages are written to sell the most expensive option. Ours start from the problem: no outlet, a low seat, no drain, a seat that shifts. Sometimes the right answer is a $150 seat instead of a $1,200 toilet, and we'll say so.

What we sell

Right now our catalog is ANZZI products. We are an independent seller, not ANZZI, and manufacturer warranties are provided by the manufacturer as described on each product page and in the product manual.

How ordering works

Online checkout is being set up. Until then, request a quote and we confirm price, stock and delivery in writing before any payment.
