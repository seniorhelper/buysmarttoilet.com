About Buy Smart Toilet

Buy Smart Toilet is an independent store run by Zach Wennstedt in Denver, Colorado. We sell smart toilets, bidet seats and accessible toilets, and we write plain guides on choosing them.

Why this store exists

Most toilet and bidet pages are written to sell the most expensive option. Ours start from the problem: no outlet, a low seat, no drain, a seat that shifts. Sometimes the right answer is a $150 seat instead of a $1,200 toilet, and we'll say so.

What we sell

Right now our catalog is ANZZI products. We are an independent seller, not ANZZI, and manufacturer warranties are provided by the manufacturer as described on each product page and in the product manual.

How ordering works

Online checkout is being set up. Until then, request a quote or call 1-888-779-2284 (7am to 8pm Mountain, 7 days), and we confirm price, stock and delivery in writing before any payment.

What we check before we quote

Most returns on toilets and bidet seats come from three mismatches, so we ask about them up front:

  • Bowl shape. Round and elongated seats are not interchangeable. Our one-minute fit checker settles it.
  • Power. Heated seats and smart toilets need a grounded, GFCI-protected outlet within reach of the cord. See smart toilet outlet requirements.
  • Delivery. Toilets ship by LTL freight to the curb, so someone needs to be home to inspect the crate. Seats ship as small parcels.

How we write the guides

Product specifications on this site come from the manufacturer's own product pages and spec sheets, and we say when a number comes from somewhere else. Where a spec isn't published, we leave it out rather than guess, and we'll get you the spec sheet for the exact SKU on request.
