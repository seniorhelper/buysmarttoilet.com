The Dia solves a problem most smart toilets ignore: what you touch. The lid opens on approach, the nozzle sterilizes itself, and flushing is a toe tap on a sensor hidden at the base. Nothing on this toilet asks for your hands.

The foot sensor is the real feature

A discreet sensor at the base means a light tap of your shoe flushes it. There's also remote flush and a side knob if you'd rather, plus automatic flush on standing. For a guest bath or a house with kids, it's the difference between a toilet handle everyone touches and one nobody does.

Cleaning that happens without you

A pre-mist sprays the bowl before each use so waste has a harder time adhering — it's a small thing that meaningfully cuts how often you're scrubbing. The nozzle runs a UVC light cycle on itself after each use. An activated charcoal deodorizer handles odor at the source.

Electrolyzed water wash

Rather than simply warming tap water, the Dia runs it through electrolysis before it reaches you. Three spray patterns — umbrella, water curtain, and spa jets — with adjustable pressure, followed by a four-speed warm dryer.

Comfort

Heated seat with four temperature settings. Soft-close lid. A soft LED night light so a 2 a.m. trip doesn't require the overhead light. Blackout flushing keeps it working through a power outage.

Fit and install

Standard 12-inch rough-in, seamless one-piece body — fewer seams means noticeably less to clean around the base. Needs a GFCI outlet within reach; confirm you have one before ordering.

Covered by ANZZI's limited lifetime warranty.
