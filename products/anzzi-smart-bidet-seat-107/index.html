The 107 is the step up from our 106 seat. Same platform, longer feature list, fifty dollars apart. If you're going to own this for a decade, it's the one we'd pick.

What the extra $50 buys

An integrated deodorizer is the headline. It filters bowl air continuously rather than leaving odor to the room. On a shared or windowless bathroom that single feature changes the experience more than any of the wash settings do.

The rest of the package

Auto open/close lid on approach. Adjustable heated seat. Gentle and targeted wash modes with variable water pressure. Warm air dryer with selectable temperature. Self-rinsing nozzle. Soft-close seat, night light, wireless remote.

Both bowl shapes

Elongated and round, both available. Measure from the midpoint between your seat bolts to the front of the rim first: about 16 to 17 inches is round, about 18 to 19 is elongated. Our fit checker will tell you.

Power required

Electric seat, GFCI outlet near the toilet. If your bathroom has no outlet, the ANZZI Minnow at $149.99 is non-electric and needs none — you'll trade away heat and drying.

Why a seat instead of a new toilet

If your current toilet flushes well and you're not remodeling, a seat gets you the bidet, warm water and heated comfort for a fraction of a full smart toilet, with no demolition and no freight delivery. Replace the toilet when the toilet is the problem — not before.
