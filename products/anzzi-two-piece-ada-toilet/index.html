Not everyone wants a toilet with a remote control. This is the one for the guest bath, the rental, the basement, or the house where you want a clean, quiet, water-efficient fixture and nothing else.

Comfort height is the point

The seat sits at ADA height rather than the standard 15 inches. Two extra inches sounds trivial until you've watched someone with a bad knee or a recent surgery try to stand up from a low toilet. If anyone in the house is over 60 or dealing with mobility, this is the single most useful spec on the page.

Elongated or round — same price

Elongated gives you a roomier, more comfortable bowl. Round saves roughly two inches of depth, which matters in a powder room where the door or a vanity is tight to the front of the bowl. Measure from the wall behind the toilet to whatever is in front of it before you choose.

The flush

1.28 GPF siphon design. If you're replacing a pre-1994 toilet running 3.5 gallons, you're cutting more than half your water per flush without giving up clearing power. Certified cUPC, CEC and DOE.

Everyday details

Soft-close seat included — no slamming lid at 6 a.m. Glazed ceramic that resists stains and scratches. Two-piece construction, which means if the tank ever cracks you replace a tank, not an entire toilet.

It's bidet-ready

The elongated version fits our bidet seats, including the ANZZI Minnow manual seat at $149.99 and the electric Smart Bidet Seat at $299. Buying this toilet plus a bidet seat lands well under a comparable all-in-one, and you can add the seat later.

Fit and shipping

Standard 12-inch rough-in. Needs no electrical. Ships from our warehouse within 24 hours of order processing.
