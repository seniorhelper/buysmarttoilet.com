The auto open/close lid is normally a feature you pay a thousand dollars for on an all-in-one smart toilet. Here it's on a seat that bolts to the toilet you already own.

Hands off from the moment you walk in

The lid senses you and opens. It closes on its own when you leave. For a shared bathroom, that's one less surface anybody touches, and it permanently ends the argument about the lid.

Comfort, adjustable

Heated seat with customizable warmth settings. Gentle and targeted wash modes with adjustable water pressure. Warm air drying with selectable temperatures, which cuts how much paper the house goes through.

Maintenance designed out

Self-rinsing nozzle cleans itself between uses. The seat is built for easy upkeep rather than hiding grime in seams. Soft-close, and a night light for after-dark trips.

Both bowl shapes, same price

Choose Elongated or Round at checkout — no price difference. Check your bowl before ordering: measure from the midpoint between the seat bolts to the front of the rim. Roughly 16 to 17 inches is round; roughly 18 to 19 inches is elongated (fit checker). Order the wrong one and it simply won't seal correctly.

Power

Electric seat, needs a GFCI outlet near the toilet. No outlet? The ANZZI Minnow manual seat at $149.99 needs none.

Compared to the step up

The ANZZI Smart Bidet Seat 107 at $349 is the same platform with a longer feature list. If you're deciding between the two, the 107 is worth the $50 unless budget is firm.
