A one-piece toilet has no seam between tank and bowl. That single fact is why people who clean their own bathrooms keep choosing them — there's no crevice at the back collecting what crevices collect.

Dual flush that isn't a gimmick

Two buttons on top: 1.1 gallons for liquid waste, 1.6 for solid. In a typical household the light flush handles the majority of uses. It's a gravity-assisted mechanism rather than a pressure-assist, which means it's quiet — pressure-assist toilets clear well but announce themselves to the whole house.

Material

RHINO ALLOY certified vitreous china. Elongated bowl with a wide seating surface. WaterSense and cUPC certified.

What's in the box

The toilet, a matching soft-close seat, and the mounting hardware. You don't need a separate seat purchase, which is worth checking when you compare this against anything else at this price — a decent soft-close seat is $40 to $60 on its own.

Fit

Standard 12-inch rough-in. No electrical required. Ships within 24 hours of order processing.

Honest note on one-piece toilets

They're heavier and more awkward to set than a two-piece, because you're carrying the whole thing as one unit instead of the bowl and tank separately. Plan on two people for the install.
