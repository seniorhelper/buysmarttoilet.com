This is the least glamorous product on the site and one of the most useful. It's a toilet seat with an 800-pound capacity and 75% more seating surface than a standard seat.

Who this is actually for

Three groups keep buying it. People who need a bariatric-rated seat and are tired of seats that flex. Caregivers setting up a bathroom for someone larger or less steady. And people who've had a seat crack under them once and never want to think about it again.

Why it holds

High-grade plastic rather than the thin molded shell on a hardware-store seat, solid stainless steel hinges instead of plastic ones, and large slip-resistant bumpers that keep the seat from shifting sideways — the sideways shift being what actually makes a seat feel unsafe.

It fits either bowl shape

Round or elongated, both. That's unusual and it's because the seat is oversized enough to cover either. Standard installation with the hardware that comes with it — under ten minutes.

Straight talk

No heat, no bidet, no electronics. It's a seat. But if the goal is making an existing bathroom safer and more comfortable for ninety-nine dollars, nothing else on this site does it faster.
